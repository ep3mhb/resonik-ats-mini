v2.45-r1
6 Nov 2026
Released

The basics for listeners and hams

  • CW mode with the true CW frequency
  • IARU Region 1 band plan on the scale, automatic LSB/USB
  • Dual clock (local and UTC), named time zones
  • Reliable clock sync at power-on
  • Battery time remaining
  • Automatic attenuation for strong AM stations
  • Browser installer and Wi-Fi updates from RESONIK
  • RESONIK boot screen and About
v2.45-r2
8 Nov 2026
Released

New menu and station tools

  • New default menu: six categories of coloured tiles
  • Full-screen settings pages
  • NCDXF/IARU beacon tracker
  • Memory scan, stops on signals
  • Signal logger to a CSV file
  • Propagation data from hamqsl.com
  • CAT control from a PC (Kenwood TS-480)
  • EiBi schedules with days, language and target
  • Knob setting: 20 or 30 clicks
  • Persian user manual (PDF)
v2.45-r3
13 Nov 2026
Released

Decoders and smarter tuning

  • CW decoder: Morse to text, full screen or as a strip
  • FT8 decoder with waterfall and message list
  • Band scanner: holds on signals, knob sets direction
  • Band Plan Auto: mode, filter and step set per band segment
  • Plan areas: all regions, Region 1, 2, 3, Iran and UAE
  • FT4 decoder on the FT8 page
  • FT8/FT4 decode even without the clock set
  • Performance setting: up to 3 times faster processor
  • Frequency lock: the knob sets the volume instead
  • Wi-Fi power setting for boards that reset
  • Fixes from other ats-mini forks

CW, FT8 and FT4 decoding use the speaker line wired to GPIO11 on V4/V4B boards.

v2.45-r4
15 Nov 2026
Released

Control from your phone or computer, Wi-Fi on the radio

  • Web Control: every setting, memories, bands and modes from the browser
  • Frequency, S-meter, RDS name and text, battery in the browser
  • Band scope: click a signal to tune to it
  • Beacons world map: who is on the air, who you hear
  • Listen to the radio in the browser (experimental, V4B)
  • Dark theme on all the radio's web pages
  • Wi-Fi on the radio: network list, on-screen keyboard, IP address shown
  • Seek in SSB and CW, on the radio and in the browser
  • Classic menu with the same six groups as the tiles
  • Faster band and mode changes
  • Beacons: names only, or off
  • Betas update to the final release over Wi-Fi

Noise reduction and sound effects

The sound goes from the receiver chip straight to the speaker, so the processor can decode signals but cannot change what you hear. For a cleaner sound use a narrower Bandwidth, SoftMute, AVC or Squelch.
