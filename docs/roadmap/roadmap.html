Decoders and smarter tuning

Noise reduction and sound effects

The sound goes straight from the receiver chip to the speaker; the processor can only listen to it. It can decode signals, but it cannot change what you hear. For a cleaner sound use a narrower Bandwidth, SoftMute, AVC or Squelch.
