**Digital** (**Menu → Ham & SWL → Digital**): RTTY (amateur 45.45 baud / 170 Hz and DWD weather 50 baud / 450 Hz), NAVTEX on 518 kHz and PSK31, decoded on the radio with a spectrum of the band. The decoder finds the signal by itself; RTTY reads normal and reversed stations alike. Click for the next mode, turn to tune, press to leave. Needs the V4B board, as the FT8 page.
