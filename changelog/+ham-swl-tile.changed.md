The **Ham & SWL** tile of the menu shows the drawing of the international amateur radio symbol in yellow: aerial, coil and earth, line for line from the symbol itself (`tools/ham-symbol.svg`, public domain), with its two straight wires shortened so that the rest is larger. It stands at the side of the tile with the name beside it. The tile had shared the mast of the Beacons tile. `tools/ham_icon.py` reduces the symbol to the tile.
