Beacons: while the followed beacon is silent (130 of every 180 seconds) the radio waits on 14.100 MHz in CW, where its next run begins, and goes there as soon as following starts. Starting is seen and heard at once, and the first of the beacon's five slots is no longer cut short by the band change. Turning through the list on the radio changes band only for the name the knob rests on.
